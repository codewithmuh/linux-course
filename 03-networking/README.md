# Module 3 — Networking (3 videos)

**Goal:** understand and troubleshoot Linux networking — the skill that separates people who *use* servers from people who can *fix* them when they can't be reached.

| # | Lesson | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | [Linux Networking](10-linux-networking/README.md) | IP, DNS, `ping`, `curl`, `wget`, `hostname` |
| 11 | [Network Troubleshooting](11-network-troubleshooting/README.md) | `ss`, `netstat`, `dig`, `nslookup`, `traceroute`, `nc` |
| 12 | [SSH & Remote Servers](12-ssh-and-remote-servers/README.md) | SSH, SSH keys, `scp`, `sftp` |

**Module outcome:** the viewer can reason about how traffic reaches a server and debug "why can't I connect?" methodically.
